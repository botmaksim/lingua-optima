# Mixed Comprehensive Grammar Review (CEFR B1)

## 1. Pedagogical Scope & Objectives
This topic forms an integral component of the CEFR B1 English curriculum. Mastery requires accurate syntactic manipulation, conceptual understanding of communicative nuance, and contextual application in authentic discourse.

## 2. Core Syntactic Structures & Rules
- **Form**: Systematic application of target grammar patterns aligned with Mixed Comprehensive Grammar Review.
- **Function**: Developing fluency, pragmatic accuracy, and register sensitivity in written and oral production.
- **Contrastive Analysis**: Learners must avoid common L1 transfer errors, irregular forms, and false cognates.

## 3. High-Frequency Collocations & Target Contexts
- Deploy lexical items characteristic of B1 proficiency.
- Ensure natural cohesion, syntactic variety, and idiomatic appropriateness.
