# Present Simple (to be & common verbs)
## Target CEFR: A1
### 1. Structural Form:
- **Verb 'to be'**:
  - Affirmative: Subject + am/is/are (e.g., *I am a student. She is ready.*)
  - Negative: Subject + am/is/are + not (e.g., *They are not at home.*)
  - Question: Am/Is/Are + Subject...? (e.g., *Are you ready?*)
- **Common Verbs**:
  - Affirmative: Subject + base verb (+ -s/-es for he/she/it). (e.g., *I work. He works.*)
  - Negative: Subject + do/does not + base verb. (e.g., *We do not like coffee.*)
  - Question: Do/Does + Subject + base verb? (e.g., *Does she live here?*)
### 2. Core Usage:
- Express permanent states, habits, routines, and universal facts.
### 3. Common Errors:
- Forgetting the 3rd person singular -s (e.g., *He work every day* -> *He works*).
- Using 'do/does' with 'to be' (e.g., *Do you are happy?* -> *Are you happy?*).
### 4. Exemplary Sentences:
- *Emma lives in London and works at a hospital.*
- *They do not speak Spanish.*
- *Is your brother an engineer?*
