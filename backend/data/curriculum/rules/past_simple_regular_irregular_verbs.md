# Past Simple (Regular & Irregular Verbs)
## Target CEFR: A2
### 1. Structural Form:
- Regular: Verb + -ed (*walk -> walked, decide -> decided*).
- Irregular: Second form (*go -> went, see -> saw, buy -> bought*).
- Negative: Subject + did not (didn't) + base verb (*I didn't see him*).
- Question: Did + subject + base verb? (*Did you go to London?*).
### 2. Common Errors:
- Double past in negative/interrogative (*Did you went?* -> *Did you go?*).
- Incorrect irregular forms (*buyed* -> *bought*).
### 3. Exemplary Sentences:
- *We visited the National Gallery yesterday.*
- *She didn't know the correct answer.*
- *Did you catch the early train this morning?*
