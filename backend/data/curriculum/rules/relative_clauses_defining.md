# Relative Clauses (defining)
## Target CEFR: B1
### 1. Structural Form:
- Relative Pronouns: *who* (people), *which* (things), *that* (people/things), *where* (places), *whose* (possession).
- Essential information defining which person/object is meant. No commas used.
- Pronoun omission: Can omit *who/which/that* when it is the OBJECT of the relative clause (*The book (which) I purchased yesterday*).
### 2. Exemplary Sentences:
- *The engineer who designed the renewable battery won an international award.*
- *This is the software that streamlined our workflow.*
