# Ellipsis and Substitution
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Pedagogical Core:
- **Ellipsis**: Economical omission of words whose meaning is retrievable from context to avoid cumbersome repetition.
  - Modal ellipsis: *She wanted to attend the conference, but couldn't [attend the conference].*
  - Verbal ellipsis: *Some colleagues supported the merger and others didn't [support the merger].*
  - Pseudogapping & Gapping: *David studied linguistics and Clara [studied] anthropology.*
- **Substitution**: Replacing phrases with pro-forms (*do so, one/ones, that/those, so, not*).
  - *Will it rain? I suspect so. / I hope not.*
  - *They promised to submit their proposals, and in doing so, demonstrated their commitment.*
