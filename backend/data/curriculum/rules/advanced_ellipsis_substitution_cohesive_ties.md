# Advanced Ellipsis, Substitution & Cohesive Ties
## Target CEFR: C2
### 1. Micro- and Macro-Level Cohesion:
- Sophisticated syntactic compression avoiding any redundant phonetic or lexical artifact.
- Zero-anaphora in complex coordinate clauses.
- Lexical cohesion through hyponymy, meronymy, and nuanced synonymic register variations.
- Ellipsis in comparative structures (*The economic consequences for developing nations will be far more acute than [they will be] for industrialized ones*).
