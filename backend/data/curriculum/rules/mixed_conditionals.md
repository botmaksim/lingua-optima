# Grammar Rule: Mixed Conditionals (Types 3-2 & 2-3)

## 1. Type 3-2: Past Unreal Condition with Present Consequence
- **Structure:** `If + Past Perfect (had + V3), would / could / might + Base Verb`
- **Meaning:** Refers to an unreal past event or action and its lingering present result or state.
- **Example:**
  - *If I had accepted the promotion in Tokyo, I would be living in Japan now.*
  - *If she had studied computer science, she wouldn't be struggling to find a tech role today.*

## 2. Type 2-3: Present/General Condition with Past Consequence
- **Structure:** `If + Past Simple (were / had / V2), would / could / might + have + V3`
- **Meaning:** Refers to a permanent personal trait, general truth, or ongoing state that influenced a specific past event.
- **Example:**
  - *If he were more punctual, he wouldn't have missed the morning briefing.*
  - *If they didn't speak fluent French, they wouldn't have secured the diplomatic contract.*

## 3. Key Inversion Variants
- *Had I known about the scheduling conflict, I wouldn't be stranded at the airport.*
- *Were she not so conscientious, she wouldn't have caught the balance sheet discrepancy.*
