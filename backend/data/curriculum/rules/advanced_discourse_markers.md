# Advanced Discourse Markers
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Categories & Pragmatic Markers:
- **Concession & Counter-argument**: *Notwithstanding, Albeit, In spite of which, By the same token, That being said, Conversely*.
- **Structuring & Digression**: *With reference to, Apropos of, As regards, To digress for a moment*.
- **Logical Deductions & Inferences**: *Consequently, Inasmuch as, Whence, It follows that*.
- **Dismissal of Previous Points**: *At all events, In any case, Be that as it may*.
### 2. Exemplary Sentences:
- *The project achieved its commercial milestones, albeit with substantial cost overruns.*
- *Notwithstanding persistent macroeconomic headwinds, the firm sustained double-digit growth.*
