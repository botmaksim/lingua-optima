# Comparatives and Superlatives (B1 Advanced Structures)
## Target CEFR: B1
### Advanced Patterns:
- Proportional comparative: *The more..., the more...* (*The more you practice, the more confident you become*).
- Progressive change: *getting better and better*, *more and more complex*.
- Degree modifiers: *significantly, considerably, slightly, far more, much less*.
- Equality & Inequality: *not nearly as... as*, *just as... as*.
