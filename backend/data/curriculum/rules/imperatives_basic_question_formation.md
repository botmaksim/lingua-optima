# Imperatives & Basic Question Formation
## Target CEFR: A1
### 1. Structural Form:
- **Imperatives**:
  - Positive: Base form of verb (*Sit down. Listen carefully.*)
  - Negative: Don't + base form (*Don't touch that. Don't worry.*)
- **Question Words (Wh-)**:
  - Wh- word + do/does/is/are + subject + verb?
  - Who, What, Where, When, Why, How.
### 2. Common Errors:
- Inverting without auxiliary (e.g., *Where you live?* -> *Where do you live?*).
- Using subject pronouns in imperatives (*You sit down!*).
### 3. Exemplary Sentences:
- *Please turn off your mobile phone during class.*
- *Where do you buy fresh bread?*
- *Why is the train delayed today?*
