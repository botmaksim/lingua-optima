# Gerunds vs Infinitives
## Target CEFR: B2
### 1. Verbs with Semantic Contrast:
- **Remember**:
  - *remember to do*: remember a duty/task before doing it (*Remember to lock the doors*).
  - *remember doing*: recall a past experience (*I remember meeting him in Vienna*).
- **Stop**:
  - *stop to do*: pause an action in order to do another (*We stopped to inspect the engine*).
  - *stop doing*: terminate an activity permanently or temporarily (*He stopped smoking*).
- **Try**:
  - *try to do*: make an effort to achieve something difficult (*I tried to lift the crate*).
  - *try doing*: experiment with a method to see if it works (*Try rebooting the server*).
- **Regret**:
  - *regret to inform*: formal announcement of bad news.
  - *regret doing*: feel remorse for past action.
