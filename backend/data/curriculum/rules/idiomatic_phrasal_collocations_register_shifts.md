# Idiomatic Phrasal Collocations & Register Shifts
## Target CEFR: C2
### 1. Pedagogical Mastery:
- Seamlessly modulating between diplomatic ambiguity, academic rigor, and razor-sharp executive brevity.
- Inflexible binominals and trinominals (*part and parcel, null and void, lock, stock, and barrel*).
- Highly specialized institutional collocations (*cast aspersions, curry favor, toe the line, rest on one's laurels, weather the storm*).
- Collocational precision (*abject failure, sweeping generalizations, consummate skill, unmitigated disaster*).
