# Participle Clauses
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Structural Types:
- **Present Participle (-ing)**: Active voice, contemporaneous actions (*Walking through the laboratory, she noticed a discrepancy*).
- **Past Participle (-ed / V3)**: Passive meaning (*Faced with dwindling resources, the director postponed the excavation*).
- **Perfect Participle (Having + V3)**: Prior completed action (*Having finalized the clinical trial, the scientists drafted their manuscript*).
- **Passive Perfect Participle (Having been + V3)**: (*Having been subjected to rigorous peer review, the study was published*).
### 2. Key Rule - Dangling Participles:
- The implied subject of the participle clause MUST match the subject of the main clause. Incorrect: *Walking down the hall, the lights flickered.*
