# Reported Speech (Advanced Reporting Verbs)
## Target CEFR: B2
### 1. Syntactic Shifts:
- Backshift of tenses (Present -> Past, Past/Present Perfect -> Past Perfect).
- Deictic time and place shifts (*now -> then, yesterday -> the previous day, here -> there*).
### 2. Verb Patterns with Advanced Reporting Verbs:
- Verb + that + clause: *insisted, admitted, claimed, argued, confirmed*.
- Verb + object + to-infinitive: *urged, persuaded, reminded, instructed*.
- Verb + preposition + -ing: *apologized for, congratulated on, insisted on*.
- Verb + -ing: *suggested, denied, recommended*.
### 3. Exemplary Sentences:
- *The chief financial officer admitted making an error in the quarterly projections.*
- *The committee urged the executive board to reconsider the proposed merger.*
