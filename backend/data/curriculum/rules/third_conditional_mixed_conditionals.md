# Third Conditional & Mixed Conditionals
## Target CEFR: B2
### 1. Structural Formulas:
- **Third Conditional (Unreal Past Condition & Unreal Past Result)**:
  - *If + had + V3, would have + V3.*
  - *If they had received the warning, they would have evacuated immediately.*
- **Mixed Conditional Type A (Past Condition with Present Consequence)**:
  - *If + had + V3, would + base verb (now).*
  - *If I had taken that software development job, I would be living in San Francisco today.*
- **Mixed Conditional Type B (Permanent Present Condition with Past Consequence)**:
  - *If + Past Simple, would have + V3.*
  - *If she were not so terrified of public speaking, she would have delivered the keynote address.*
