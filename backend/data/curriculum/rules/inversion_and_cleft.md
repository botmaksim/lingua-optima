# Grammar Rule: Advanced Inversion & Cleft Sentences

## 1. Negative Adverbial Inversion
- **Structure:** `Negative Adverbial + Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb`
- **Trigger Expressions:**
  - *Not only... but also...*
  - *Hardly / Scarcely... when...*
  - *No sooner... than...*
  - *Seldom / Rarely / Little...*
  - *Under no circumstances / On no account...*
- **Example:**
  - *Not only did the initiative reduce operational overhead, but it also bolstered staff retention.*
  - *Seldom have international delegates encountered such unanimous consensus.*

## 2. Cleft Sentences for Focus & Emphasis
- **It-Cleft:** `It + be (is/was) + Focused Element + that/who + Clause`
  - *It was the implementation phase that caused the unexpected bottleneck.*
- **Wh-Cleft (Pseudo-cleft):** `What + Subject + Verb + is/was + Complement`
  - *What the board prioritized was sustainable long-term dividend growth.*
  - *All that remains to be finalized is the compliance sign-off.*
