# Inversion with Negative Adverbials
## Target CEFR: B2
### 1. Structural Form:
- Negative/Restrictive Adverbial + Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb.
- Key Adverbials: *Seldom, Rarely, Never before, Little, Scarcely... when, No sooner... than, Hardley... when, Under no circumstances, On no account, At no time*.
### 2. Exemplary Patterns:
- *Seldom have we witnessed such remarkable resilience.*
- *Under no circumstances should confidential passwords be shared.*
- *Little did the executives realize that the market was on the verge of collapsing.*
- *No sooner had the presentation concluded than the questions began.*
