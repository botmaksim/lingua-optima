# Nuanced Modal Idioms
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Complex Modal Idioms:
- *May/Might as well*: Suggesting the most sensible option because there is no better alternative (*We might as well proceed with the backup plan*).
- *Might have known*: Expressing ironical lack of surprise (*I might have known he would forget the meeting*).
- *Cannot but / cannot help but*: Unavoidable compulsion (*One cannot but admire their unwavering perseverance*).
- *Would sooner / Would rather*: Strong preference (*She would sooner resign than compromise her ethical standards*).
- *Dare say*: Tentative opinion (*I dare say the negotiations will stall over intellectual property rights*).
