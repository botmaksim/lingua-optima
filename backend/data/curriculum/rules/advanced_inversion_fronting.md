# Advanced Inversion & Fronting
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Syntactic Structures:
- **Negative & Limiting Adverbials**: *Not only... but also, Not until, Only after, Only when, In no way*.
  - *Not until the final audit was published did the stakeholders comprehend the financial shortfall.*
- **Fronted Negative Participles & Comparative Adjectives**:
  - *Such was the velocity of the storm that trees were uprooted.*
  - *So intricate was the algorithm that few engineers could decipher it.*
- **Locative & Directional Inversion**:
  - *Into the boardroom walked the chief mediator.*
  - *On the summit stands an ancient observatory.*
### 2. Pragmatic Function:
- Heightens rhetorical drama, creates emphatic stylistic focus, and elevates text register to academic/literary elegance.
