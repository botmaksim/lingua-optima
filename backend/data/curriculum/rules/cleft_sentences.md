# Cleft Sentences (It-Clefts & Wh-Clefts)
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Typology:
- **It-Cleft**: *It + be + focused element + who/that + clause*.
  - *It was Margaret who identified the flaw in the thermodynamic model.*
  - *It was only after extensive trials that the drug received regulatory approval.*
- **Wh- / Pseudo-Cleft**: *What / All + clause + be + focused element*.
  - *What surprised the research team was the unexpected stability of the compound.*
  - *All the project needs is adequate computational capacity.*
- **Reverse Pseudo-Cleft**:
  - *Financial autonomy is what the burgeoning institution seeks.*
### 2. Stylistic Function:
- Focalizes precise information, resolves ambiguity, and creates contrastive emphasis in scholarly or formal exposition.
