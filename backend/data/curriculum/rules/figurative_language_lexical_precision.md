# Figurative Language & Lexical Precision
## Target CEFR: C2
### 1. Mastery of Metaphor, Irony & Stance:
- Conceptual metaphors structuring abstract intellectual discourse.
- Litotes (understatement via double negative: *not entirely without merit, a not insignificant proportion*).
- Synecdoche and metonymy (*the corridors of power, the pen is mightier than the sword*).
- Hyperbolic and ironic stances employed with crystalline pragmatic intention.
