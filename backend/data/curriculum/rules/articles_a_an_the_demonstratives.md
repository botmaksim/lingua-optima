# Articles (a, an, the) & Demonstratives
## Target CEFR: A1
### 1. Structural Form:
- **Indefinite Article (a/an)**:
  - 'a' before consonant sounds (*a car, a university*).
  - 'an' before vowel sounds (*an apple, an hour*).
- **Definite Article (the)**:
  - Specific, previously mentioned, or unique items (*the sun, the teacher*).
- **Demonstratives**:
  - Singular: *this* (near), *that* (far).
  - Plural: *these* (near), *those* (far).
### 2. Core Usage:
- Introduce singular countable nouns for the first time; identify proximity of objects.
### 3. Common Errors:
- Using 'a/an' with plural or uncountable nouns (*an apples* -> *apples*).
- Confusing phonetic vowel sound with vowel letter (*a umbrella* -> *an umbrella*).
### 4. Exemplary Sentences:
- *This is an interesting book on the table.*
- *Look at those birds in the park.*
- *I need a pencil and an eraser.*
