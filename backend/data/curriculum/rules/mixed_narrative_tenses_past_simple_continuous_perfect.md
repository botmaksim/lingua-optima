# Mixed Narrative Tenses: Past Simple, Continuous & Perfect
## Target CEFR: B1
### Integrated Challenge:
- Orchestrating multi-layered narratives:
  - *Past Continuous*: Background actions in progress.
  - *Past Simple*: Main sequential chronological events.
  - *Past Perfect*: Flashbacks / events that occurred prior to the narrative focal point.
### Model Pattern:
- *When the inspector arrived (Past Simple), the detective was examining (Past Continuous) the safe which the burglars had broken into (Past Perfect).*
