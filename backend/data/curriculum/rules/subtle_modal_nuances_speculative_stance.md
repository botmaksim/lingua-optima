# Subtle Modal Nuances & Speculative Stance
## Target CEFR: C2
### 1. Epistemic Modulation:
- Employing fine distinctions in epistemic hedging (*might well, could conceivably, is arguably, stands to reason*).
- Epistemic distance in scholarly texts (*would seem to indicate, might be interpreted as, points tentatively toward*).
- Counter-inductive nuance: *Doubtless they believed their methodology sound, yet...*
- Evidential verbs coupled with speculative adverbs (*purportedly demonstrates, ostensibly suggests*).
