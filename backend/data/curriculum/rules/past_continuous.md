# Past Continuous
## Target CEFR: B1
### 1. Structural Form:
- Subject + was/were + verb-ing.
### 2. Core Usage:
- Background descriptions in narratives (*The wind was blowing and rain was falling*).
- An ongoing past action interrupted by a shorter action in Past Simple (*While I was walking home, my phone rang*).
- Two parallel actions occurring simultaneously (*While Mark was cooking, Julia was setting the table*).
### 3. Common Errors:
- Using past continuous with stative verbs (*I was knowing him* -> *I knew him*).
