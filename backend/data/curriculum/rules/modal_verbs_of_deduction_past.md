# Modal Verbs of Deduction (Past)
## Target CEFR: B2
### 1. Epistemic Probability in the Past:
- **Certainty (Positive)**: *must have + V3* (*The ground is soaked; it must have rained heavily*).
- **Impossibility (Negative Certainty)**: *can't have / couldn't have + V3* (*He can't have committed the forgery; he was abroad*).
- **Possibility / Speculation**: *might have / may have / could have + V3* (*The shipment may have been delayed at customs*).
- **Unrealized Opportunity**: *could have + V3* (*You could have called to let us know*).
- **Criticism of Past Action**: *should have + V3* (*They should have backed up the database*).
