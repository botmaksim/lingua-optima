# Mixed A1 Review: Present Simple, Pronouns & Basic Prepositions
## Target CEFR: A1
### Integrated Challenge:
- Combining present simple verb agreements, subject/object/possessive pronouns, and time/place prepositions into cohesive statements.
### Model Pattern:
- *My sister (possessive) works (3rd person -s) at a hospital (preposition of place) in the evening (preposition of time).*
