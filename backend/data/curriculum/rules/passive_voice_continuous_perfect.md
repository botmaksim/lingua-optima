# Passive Voice (Continuous & Perfect)
## Target CEFR: B2
### 1. Structural Formulas:
- Present Continuous Passive: *am/is/are + being + V3* (*The servers are being upgraded*).
- Past Continuous Passive: *was/were + being + V3* (*The negotiations were being finalized*).
- Present Perfect Passive: *have/has + been + V3* (*The regulations have been amended*).
- Past Perfect Passive: *had + been + V3* (*The contract had been signed prior to the merger*).
- Modal Passive: *modal + be/have been + V3* (*The data must be safeguarded*).
### 2. Pragmatic Tone:
- Essential in formal academic, corporate, legal, and journalism registers to maintain institutional objectivity.
