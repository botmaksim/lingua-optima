# Comparative and Superlative Adjectives
## Target CEFR: A2
### 1. Structural Form:
- Short adjectives:
  - Comparative: adj + -er + than (*taller than, faster than*).
  - Superlative: the + adj + -est (*the tallest, the fastest*).
- Long adjectives:
  - Comparative: more + adj + than (*more expensive than*).
  - Superlative: the most + adj (*the most expensive*).
- Irregulars:
  - good -> better -> the best
  - bad -> worse -> the worst
  - far -> farther/further -> the farthest/furthest
### 2. Common Errors:
- Double comparatives (*more taller*).
- Omitting 'the' before superlatives (*He is best player* -> *He is the best player*).
