# Future with 'Going to' vs 'Will'
## Target CEFR: A2
### 1. Structural Form & Usage:
- **Be going to + base verb**:
  - Pre-planned intentions/decisions (*I'm going to study medicine next year*).
  - Predictions based on visible evidence (*Look at those clouds; it's going to rain*).
- **Will + base verb**:
  - Spontaneous decisions made at the moment of speaking (*I'll help you with that bag*).
  - General predictions / opinions with think, believe (*I think they will win*).
### 2. Exemplary Sentences:
- *Are you going to attend the seminar tomorrow?*
- *Wait, I'll hold the elevator door for you.*
- *Scientists believe electric transport will dominate the market.*
