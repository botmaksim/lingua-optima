# Present Perfect vs Past Simple
## Target CEFR: B1
### 1. Structural Form & Distinct Semantics:
- **Past Simple**: Finished action at a specified past time (*in 2019, yesterday, when I was young*).
  - Form: Subject + V2.
- **Present Perfect**: Action connected to the present, life experiences without specific time, uncompleted time periods (*today, this year*), or duration with *for/since*.
  - Form: Subject + have/has + V3 (past participle).
### 2. Signal Words:
- Past Simple: *yesterday, ago, in 1995, last month, when*.
- Present Perfect: *already, yet, ever, never, just, so far, recently, for, since*.
### 3. Exemplary Sentences:
- *I lived in Paris for two years when I was a student (no longer there).*
- *I have lived in Berlin for five years (still living there).*
- *Have you ever negotiated an international contract? Yes, I negotiated one last November.*
