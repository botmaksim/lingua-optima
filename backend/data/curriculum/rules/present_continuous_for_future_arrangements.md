# Present Continuous for Future Arrangements
## Target CEFR: A2
### 1. Structural Form & Usage:
- Form: Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing + future time marker.
- Usage: Confirmed personal arrangements involving a specific time, place, or other people (*I'm seeing my dentist on Tuesday afternoon*).
### 2. Comparison:
- Present Continuous = Fixed arrangement (*I'm meeting Sarah at 7*).
- Be going to = General intention (*I'm going to meet her sometime this week*).
