# Passive Voice (Present & Past Simple)
## Target CEFR: B1
### 1. Structural Form:
- Present Simple Passive: Subject + am/is/are + V3 (past participle).
  - *Millions of emails are sent every minute.*
- Past Simple Passive: Subject + was/were + V3.
  - *The architectural masterpiece was constructed in 1889.*
### 2. Pragmatic Function:
- Shifts focus from the agent/actor to the action, process, or receiver of the action. Agent introduced with *by* only when relevant.
### 3. Common Errors:
- Omitting auxiliary 'to be' (*The car repaired yesterday* -> *was repaired*).
