# Basic Prepositions of Place & Time (in, at, on)
## Target CEFR: A1
### 1. Structural Form:
- **Time**:
  - *at*: exact times, night, weekend (*at 5 o'clock, at night*).
  - *on*: days, dates (*on Monday, on May 4th*).
  - *in*: months, years, seasons, parts of day (*in July, in 2024, in the morning*).
- **Place**:
  - *at*: specific points (*at home, at the bus stop*).
  - *on*: surfaces (*on the wall, on the floor*).
  - *in*: enclosed spaces, cities, countries (*in the room, in London*).
### 2. Common Errors:
- Saying *in Monday* instead of *on Monday*.
- Saying *at the morning* instead of *in the morning*.
### 3. Exemplary Sentences:
- *The meeting is on Friday at 9:00 AM.*
- *My keys are on the desk in the kitchen.*
- *We go on vacation in July.*
