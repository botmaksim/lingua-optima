# Have to & Must (Basic Rules)
## Target CEFR: A2
### 1. Structural Form & Differences:
- **Must + base verb**: Strong personal obligation, internal rule, or official written regulation (*You must sign the register*).
- **Have to + base verb**: External obligation, objective requirement (*I have to wake up at 6 AM for work*).
- **Negative Contrast (Crucial)**:
  - *Mustn't*: Prohibition (*You mustn't park here - it's illegal*).
  - *Don't have to*: Absence of obligation (*You don't have to wear a suit - it's casual*).
### 2. Exemplary Sentences:
- *Visitors must wear identification badges at all times.*
- *You don't have to pay for entry today; admission is free.*
