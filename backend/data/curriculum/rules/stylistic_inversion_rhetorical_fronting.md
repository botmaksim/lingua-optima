# Stylistic Inversion & Rhetorical Fronting
## Target CEFR: C2
### 1. Literary & Rhetorical Constructs:
- **Predicate Fronting**: *Gone are the days when companies could ignore environmental sustainability.*
- **Comparative Fronting with Inversion**: *Greater still was their astonishment when the results replicated.*
- **Participial Fronting with Verb Inversion**: *Hanging over the negotiation was the perpetual threat of regulatory intervention.*
- **Chiasmus & Parallel Syntax**: *Ask not what your country can do for you; ask what you can do for your country.*
### 2. Stylistic Mastery:
- Utilized in high-stakes oratorical discourse, supreme court opinions, and literary essays for maximum emotional gravitas.
