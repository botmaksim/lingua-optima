# Modal Verbs (obligation, permission, advice)
## Target CEFR: B1
### 1. Functions & Modals:
- **Obligation**: *must* (internal/strong), *have to* (external requirement), *need to* (necessity).
- **Prohibition**: *mustn't* (strictly forbidden).
- **No Obligation**: *don't have to, needn't* (optional).
- **Advice & Recommendation**: *should, ought to, had better* (urgent advice with implied consequence).
- **Permission**: *can, could (polite), may (formal)*.
### 2. Exemplary Sentences:
- *You should review the contract thoroughly before signing.*
- *Applicants must possess a bachelor's degree in engineering.*
