# Possessive Adjectives & Possessive 's
## Target CEFR: A1
### 1. Structural Form:
- **Possessive Adjectives**: my, your, his, her, its, our, their + noun.
- **Saxon Genitive ('s)**:
  - Singular noun: noun + 's (*John's car, the teacher's desk*).
  - Regular plural noun: noun ending in -s + ' (*the students' books*).
  - Irregular plural noun: noun + 's (*the children's toys*).
### 2. Common Errors:
- Confusing *its* (possessive) and *it's* (it is).
- Confusing *their* (possessive), *there* (place), and *they're* (they are).
### 3. Exemplary Sentences:
- *This is Maria's laptop and that is her backpack.*
- *Our parents live in a quiet neighborhood.*
- *The children's room is clean and bright.*
