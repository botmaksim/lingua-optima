# Subjunctive Mood (Mandative & Formulaic)
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Structural Form:
- **Mandative Subjunctive**: Base form of the verb (bare infinitive) in that-clauses following verbs/adjectives of urgency, demand, or recommendation.
  - Verbs: *demand, insist, recommend, propose, require, suggest, urge*.
  - Adjectives: *essential, vital, imperative, crucial, desirable*.
  - Structure: *It is imperative that every candidate be evaluated impartially.*
  - Negative: *We recommend that he not disclose the proprietary patent.*
  - Passive: *The court ordered that the prisoner be released.*
- **Formulaic Subjunctive**:
  - *Be that as it may*, *Come what may*, *Suffice it to say*, *Heaven forbid*, *God save the Queen*.
### 2. Common Errors:
- Conjugating for 3rd person singular (*that he arrives on time* instead of subjunctive *that he arrive on time*).
