# Hypothetical Meaning & Unreal Past
## Target CEFR: C1
### 1. Structural Patterns:
- **It's (high / about) time + Past Simple**: Expresses that an action should have been taken already (*It is high time governments addressed algorithmic bias*).
- **Would rather / Would sooner + Subject + Past Simple**: Preference regarding someone else's action (*I would rather you didn't mention this disclosure to the board*).
- **As if / As though + Unreal Past**: Unreal or counter-factual comparison (*He spoke about quantum physics as though he had discovered the principles himself*).
- **Suppose / Supposing / What if**: Speculative exploration (*Supposing we missed the deadline, what contingency plan exists?*).
